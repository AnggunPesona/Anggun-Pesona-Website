# Booking Notes — "A few gentle notes 🤍"

Reference copy for the booking panel notes (`.bk-disc` block in `track/index.html`).

## Plain copy

1. Once you've booked, kindly **wait for our confirmation text** before heading over — item prepared outside for smooth, contactless pickup.
2. Please **don't drop by unannounced** — unbooked orders may not be ready.
3. Arrange your slot **at least 12 hours in advance**.
4. After booking, you can **check your booking confirmation here on the tracker**.
5. Self-collect: our team sends **collection details on the day (or a day before)** + a confirmation text that your order is ready.
6. Delivery: the time is **tentative**, updated and confirmed on the day based on our routes.
