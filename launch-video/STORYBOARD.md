## If you want to assemble this manually in CapCut

1. Take the HTML animation, screen-record it (see `HOW-TO-RECORD.md`)
2. Import the recording into CapCut
3. Add music — sync the "drop" to Scene 2 (the logo reveal)
4. **Optional:** add a sticker tap-sound effect when stickers pop in (CapCut → Sound Effects → "pop" or "tap")
5. **Optional:** add a soft "whoosh" when each offering card slides in
6. Add a 0.5s fade-to-black at the very end before looping
7. Export at 1080p, 30fps, MP4

## If you want to skip CapCut entirely

The screen recording IS your final video — just trim the start/end and post it directly. No editing needed. The animation already includes all the motion, text, and pacing.
