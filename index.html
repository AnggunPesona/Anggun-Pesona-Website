About Anggun Pesona

Our story, our values, our obsession with beautiful shoes

Born from a Love for Shoes

Anggun Pesona — graceful allure in Malay — was born from a simple belief: the right pair of shoes can transform how you carry yourself. We're a Brunei-based curated footwear brand passionate about sourcing chic, versatile pieces that elevate your everyday and honour your most special moments.

Whether you're building a capsule shoe wardrobe, hunting for the perfect bridal pair, or simply treating yourself to something gorgeous — we curate with intention. Every piece is hand-selected for its quality, wearability, and that unmistakable something-special.

We operate on a monthly batch pre-order model, giving you access to carefully sourced styles before they sell out. Our limited in-stocks offer immediate pieces for those spontaneous moments when you simply need those shoes right now.
